Banana Biscuit Bites
a. Ingredients (with proportion):

12 plain biscuits (6 pairs), 1 medium banana (12 round slices), 3 tbsp peanut butter, 2 tbsp crushed cornflakes (optional for coating).

b. Detailed recipe:

Spread peanut butter on one biscuit, place a banana slice on top, cover with another biscuit to make a sandwich, roll sides in crushed cornflakes if desired, and repeat for all pieces.

c. Estimated time taken to prepare:

Approximately 10–12 minutes.

d. Approx quantity:

Makes 6 banana biscuit sandwiches and can serve 2–3 people.

e. Calorie and related information:

Approximately 90–110 kcal per piece, containing 12–15 g carbohydrates, 2–3 g protein, and 4–6 g fat.
