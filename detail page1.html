Fruit & Nut Trail Mix
a. Ingredients (with proportion):

1 medium banana (sliced), 6–8 strawberries (halved), 1 small orange (segmented), ¼ cup almonds, 2 tbsp sunflower seeds, optional 1 tbsp honey.

b. Detailed recipe:

Wash and cut all fruits, arrange them in a bowl, add almonds and sunflower seeds on top, drizzle honey if desired, mix lightly, and serve fresh.

c. Estimated time taken to prepare:

Approximately 8–10 minutes.

d. Approx quantity:

Serves 2 people as a healthy snack or light breakfast.

e. Calorie and related information:

Approximately 280–320 kcal per serving, containing 30–35 g carbohydrates, 7–9 g protein, 15–18 g healthy fats, and 6–8 g fiber.
