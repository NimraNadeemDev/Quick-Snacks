TOP SOURCES FOR FRESH AND HEALTHY ITEMS
ALMOND

Almond are versatile nuts used in snacks like trail mixes, granola bars, and desserts.Almonds are rich in healthy fats, protein, vitamins etc.They are widely available in supermarkets, health stores, and online grocery stores.

PUMKIN SEEDS

These seeds are commonly used in granola, trail mixes, and energy bars for a crunchy texture.Best purchased from supermarkets, health food stores, or online grocery shops. Rich in protein, healthy fats, and minerals etc and great for heart.

KIWI FRUIT

Kiwi is often used in fruit salads, smoothies, and yogurt bowls for a tangy flavor. It pairs well with granola, berries, and nuts. Best purchased from supermarkets, fresh fruit markets, or online grocery stores. Rich in vitamin C,and it supports digestion.

MILK

Milk is commonly used in smoothies, puddings, cakes, and creamy desserts. It pairs well with cereal, cocoa, and fruits in snacks. Best purchased from supermarkets, local dairies, or online grocery stores. Rich in calcium, protein, and vitamins, it supports bone and overall health.

PEANUT BUTTER

Peanut butter is used in sandwiches, smoothies, and cookies for creamy flavor and protein. It pairs well with bread, fruits, and oats in snacks. Best purchased from supermarkets, health food stores, or online grocery shops. Rich in healthy protein, and vitamins, it supports energy and muscle health.

BLUE BERRIES

Blueberries are commonly used in smoothies, yogurt bowls, muffins, and fruit salads for natural sweetness. They pair well with oats, nuts etc. Best purchased from supermarkets, fresh fruit markets, or online grocery stores. Rich in vitamins, and fiber, they support immunity and heart health.
