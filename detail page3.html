Coffee
a. Ingredients – Measured items needed

Coffee powder – 2 tsp, Water – 1 cup, Milk – 1 cup, Sugar – 2 tsp (optional)

b. Recipe – Simple preparation steps.

Boil water, add coffee and mix. Heat milk separately, combine with coffee, add sugar, stir and serve hot.

c. Time – Total preparation time.

5 minutes

d. Quantity – Number of servings.

Serves 2 cups

e. Calories – Approximate nutrition per cup.

90–120 kcal (depends on milk & sugar)
