Teddy Bear Peanut Butter Banana Toast
a. Ingredients (with proportion):

3 slices bread, 3 tbsp peanut butter (1 tbsp per slice), 1 large banana (9 round slices), 9 blueberries, 1–2 tsp honey (optional).

b. Detailed recipe:

Lightly toast the bread, spread 1 tbsp peanut butter on each slice, place 2 banana slices on top corners for ears and 1 slice in the center for the snout, add 2 blueberries for eyes and 1 blueberry for the nose, drizzle honey if desired, and serve immediately.

c. Estimated time taken to prepare:

Approximately 10–12 minutes (5–7 minutes preparation + 3–5 minutes toasting).

d. Approx quantity:

Makes 3 teddy bear toasts and can serve 2–3 people.

e. Calorie and related information:

Approximately 220–250 kcal per toast, containing around 28–32 g carbohydrates, 7–9 g protein, 10–12 g fat, and 3–4 g fiber.
